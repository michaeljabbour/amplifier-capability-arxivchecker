ArxivChecker: Trace. Rebuild. Review.

Silent text film. All meaning is conveyed by the on-screen text. No narration or music.
Figures are illustrations, not study results.

Your study is done. The paper is not.
Start with the design, methods, raw results and draft.
Inventory the study before writing.

Every claim needs a route to evidence.
Keep the source, denominator, unit and cost coverage together.
Trace every claim to evidence and keep its limits visible.

Build the figure. Then look at it.
Missing series should fail loudly. Inspect labels, scales and panels.
Rebuild figures from data, then inspect every figure.

Tell the whole story. Check every reference.
Problem, design, harness, test, results and limits—in that order.
Explain the system and its test; verify reference details.

Rebuild from the submission package.
Check the build, fonts, filenames and all required source files.
Test a clean build from the actual submission package.

A person makes the final call.
Review authorship, AI use, category, licence and release.
Human review remains essential. ArxivChecker does not guarantee acceptance.
