ArxivChecker’s local profile

The template’s layout and preferred fonts are local choices. Visible page numbers are the house default unless the author or venue calls for another treatment; this is not an arXiv requirement. The helper’s clean-build, font and filename checks cover a defined local profile; alternative supported workflows need their own relevant checks.

Passing the local profile does not certify arXiv acceptance. Failing a local-profile check does not by itself establish that arXiv would reject the paper.

LaTeX recommendations

Use source that describes the structure: standard title and author blocks, meaningful headings and useful image alternatives. These support readable HTML and accessibility.

arXiv’s LaTeX best practices are recommendations, not additional universal acceptance gates. Class and package guidance explains typesetting mechanisms.

MJ’s publishing style

Teach the reader the study. Explain what was built before reporting its results. Define useful technical terms, use short footnotes for secondary detail, and choose visuals that explain a question.

ArxivChecker prepares evidence and a reviewable package. It cannot guarantee scientific validity, authorship, arXiv acceptance, endorsement or a particular category.

Formatting preferences are separate from arXiv requirements. Consult the current TeX submission guidance and AI policy when preparing an actual submission. Choose the disclosure and license that accurately fit the work.
