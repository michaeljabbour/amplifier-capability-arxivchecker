ArxivChecker’s local profile

The template’s layout, preferred fonts and omission of page numbers are local choices. The helper’s clean-build, font and filename checks cover a defined local profile; alternative supported workflows need their own relevant checks.

Passing the local profile does not certify arXiv acceptance. Failing a local-profile check does not by itself establish that arXiv would reject the paper.

ArxivChecker prepares evidence and a reviewable package. It cannot guarantee scientific validity, authorship, arXiv acceptance, endorsement or a particular category.

Formatting preferences are separate from arXiv requirements. Consult the current TeX submission guidance and AI policy when preparing an actual submission. Choose the disclosure and license that accurately fit the work.

Study files remain local unless you choose to share them with your assistant or another service. This static website has no upload form, analytics or backend.
