# Verification of supplied policy guidance

**Official sources checked: 2026-11-03 (UTC).** The owner supplied a secondary summary of publishing guidance. It was treated as a set of claims to verify, not as policy authority. No private attachment text, identifiers, links or unrelated material are redistributed here.

The portable canonical sources are the [requirements map](../skills/arxivchecker/references/requirements.md) and [submission map](../skills/arxivchecker/references/submission.md#content-type-and-account-checks). This record explains the verification outcome without duplicating those maps.

| Claim area | Outcome | Primary evidence and scope |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Monthly/active quotas and rejected-submission counting | Verified, with accounting conditions | [Official rate-policy FAQ](https://blog.arxiv.org/2026/10/01/updated-rate-limit-policy/) is dated/effective 2026-10-01. It describes two per calendar month, three active, rejection counting, submitter-only accounting across categories and deletion/on-hold exceptions. [Current moderation](https://info.arxiv.org/help/moderation/index.html#submission-rate) calls the monthly quota new submissions. Actual available capacity is unverified without the submitter's account. |
| Replacement counting | Partly specified; do not infer a blanket exemption | [Replacement guidance](https://info.arxiv.org/help/replace.html#to-replace-an-article) gives a separate weekly rule enforced after version 5. The rate FAQ does not explicitly answer all interactions between replacements and active-quota accounting. |
| Prior peer review for CS reviews/surveys and position papers | Verified, conditional on category/content type | [Official CS announcement](https://blog.arxiv.org/2025/10/31/attention-authors-updated-practice-for-review-articles-and-position-papers-in-arxiv-cs-category/) requires accepted refereed-venue status and successful peer-review documentation. It does not impose this practice on original scientific research. Workshop review is described as generally insufficient. |
| Original, substantive, self-contained scholarship | Verified policy expectation; outcome needs expert judgement | [Moderation](https://info.arxiv.org/help/moderation/index.html) and [content-type policy](https://info.arxiv.org/help/policies/content-types.html) govern suitability. Good prose, a successful compile and a complete archive do not establish novelty, significance or acceptance. |
| Machine readability and basic typography | Verified as stated format requirements | [Format policy](https://info.arxiv.org/help/policies/format_requirements.html) provides the relevant elements, single-spacing, type-size and margin guidance and excluded features. Its `should` wording and moderation discretion are preserved in the canonical map. |
| Semantic markup, frontmatter, alt text and HTML conversion support | Verified as recommendations | [Official LaTeX markup guidance](https://info.arxiv.org/help/submit_latex_best_practices.html) labels these as best practices for accessible HTML, not additional universal rejection conditions. |
| One universal class, column count, page-number convention or bibliography workflow | Not established as a general rule | [Format policy](https://info.arxiv.org/help/policies/format_requirements.html), [processor choices](https://info.arxiv.org/help/faq/texlive.html) and [bibliography instructions](https://info.arxiv.org/help/submit_tex.html#include-bib-or-bbl-files-if-you-use-bibtexbiber) require route-aware preparation. The supplied starter's particular choices remain local defaults. |

The two policy blog posts were successfully read from official arXiv pages; intermittent retrieval errors were not treated as evidence that a policy was false. Secondary search snippets and discussion threads are not the evidence for the conclusions above. No actual submission, account-quota inspection or moderation test was performed.
